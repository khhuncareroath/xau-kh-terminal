      When a signal arrives

      1. Enter within 15 minutes at the price shown. Later than that, skip it.
      2. Set SL and TP right away. Never trade without the SL.
      3. Lot size = your risk. SL 25 points × 0.02 lot ≈ $50. Pick the lot so a loss is 1% (✅) or 0.5% (🟡) of your account.
      4. 🎯 TP1 hit: close half, move SL to entry. The rest can't lose.
      5. Result reply (🏆 / ✅ / ❌) closes the trade. Then wait for the next one.

      Don't follow when…

      • 📰 Big news is within 15 minutes
      • ⏱ The signal is older than 15 minutes, or price already moved far
      • 🟡 The plan says skipped, expired or don't chase
      • 😤 You just lost and want it back fast (revenge trading)
      • 💸 You'd risk more than 1–2% in total across open trades
